Queen Checkmate

The Queen Checkmate is the most fundamental endgame every chess player must know how to do. It is typically achieved from endgames that still have pawns, and a pawn ends up winning a pawn race to promote to a queen. All that remains to be done is to checkmate the opponent with the newly promoted queen. This page will teach you exactly how to do that with a very simple and easy-to-learn approach.


Firstly, as the queen is a very powerful piece that can move in all directions, the initial setup of the position does not really matter because we can always manipulate it into a setup that we want.


To accomplish this checkmate, we will wish to achieve the following steps:

    When it is your move, place your queen in a position that is a "knight move" away from the opponent's king
    Wherever the king moves, make the same move with your king until the king reaches the corner
    When your opponent moves their king to the corner, do NOT move your queen anymore. Instead, move your king up towards the corner the opponent's king is at
    Once your king has been brought up and is a "knight move" away from the corner the opponent's king has been trapped in, move your queen in front of the king to checkmate

The steps to checkmating may be hard to understand without direct implementation in a real scenario. Let us consider the example below:


Figure 1: White to Move


Let us consider the steps above. In this position, we can play 1.Qf4 or 1.Qf6 to put the queen a "knight" move away from black's king. Let us play 1.Qf4 to achieve the following position:


Figure 1.1: Position after 1.Qf4


Notice how the queen cuts off the black king completely and it is stuck in a box. Now, we simply follow every one of black's moves with our queen, until the black king reaches the corner, to maintain the "knight" distance with the king. This will end up closing the box until black's king is stuck in the corner and can only move between two squares. We will be able to do this no matter what black does, so let us consider the following line: 1...Kc5 2.Qe4 Kd6 3.Qf5 Kc6 4.Qe5 Kb6 5.Qd5 Kc7 6.Qe6 Kb7 7.Qd6 Ka7 8.Qc6 Kb8 9.Qd7 Ka8. With much effort, we have finally trapped the black king in the corner.


Figure 1.2: Position after 9...Ka8


We have reached the final step of the queen checkmate, as black's king is now stuck in the corner (with only two squares to move back and forth between). Now, to finally execute the checkmate, we must bring up the king: 10.Kb4 Kb8 11.Kc5 Ka8 12.Kb6 Kb8.


Figure 1.3: Position after 12...Kb8


After accomplishing all the steps, the final checkmate may be delivered with 13.Qb7#, and the game is over!
